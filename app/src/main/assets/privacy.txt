Lumina Call Guard Plus • Privacy
Lumina Studios LLC processes phone numbers on your device to identify or block calls. The app has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-operated server. The bundled FTC data contains unverified consumer reports from files published October 30–November 12, 2026. Caller ID can be spoofed.
Android: When enabled as the call screening app, Call Guard checks eligible incoming numbers and carrier verification locally. You can opt in to saving the last 50 screened calls, including time, number and decision. Turn off Save call history to stop saving new entries. Clear call history removes saved entries and recording links. Call Guard can open a recording you select through the system file picker. The original stays with its file provider. Personal rules stay until you remove them or clear app data.
iPhone: The app supplies reported-number labels and the spam numbers you personally confirm to iOS Call Directory. The list is shared on this device between the app and its extension. Remove a number in the app to update the block list. Disable the extension in Settings to turn protection off.
Reporting is optional. Opening the FTC form takes you to an external website. On Android, Copy & open FTC copies call details to your clipboard. Review the details and submit them yourself. Your browser, phone service, system Phone app and selected file provider have their own privacy practices.

Lumina Focus Preview private chat: pairing codes contain connection details and are exchanged using a channel you choose. The two devices send encrypted text and resized photos directly over WebRTC. Chat content is kept in memory. Unopened items expire after two minutes and opened items after ten seconds. Original photos remain with the sender. Other websites opened in the protected browser receive ordinary browser requests and can use browser storage.
